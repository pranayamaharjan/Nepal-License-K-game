Real-World Nepal DoTM Scooter Tips
Practical advice for passing Category K at Ekantakuna, Radhe Radhe, or Chabahil
1. Start Zone (Pre-Ride): Always buckle your helmet strap audibly, physically touch and adjust both mirrors, and kick up the side stand before twisting the throttle. Examiners watch your hands.
2. Figure-8 (Sightline & Rear Brake): Never stare at the yellow line right under your front tire—look 2–3 meters ahead toward the center crossover and exit. Keep gentle, steady throttle and modulate speed with the left (rear) brake so the scooter stays balanced and doesn't wobble.
3. Narrow Plank (Fatta): Line up straight before the ramp lip. Keep your eyes locked on the far end of the plank (not down at the wood) and maintain a steady walking pace (~8–10 km/h). Never grab the front brake on the plank.
4. U-Turn & Blinker: Right after dropping off the plank, thumb the Right Indicator before entering the curve. After straightening out, immediately cancel the indicator—leaving it blinking costs points.
5. Traffic Light: Stop cleanly behind the white stop line on Red or Yellow. Keep your left foot down for stability and wait for Green.
6. Speedbreakers (Bumps): Roll over all 4 bumps slowly (<10–12 km/h) with light, steady throttle. Pulling the right (front) brake over a bump compresses the fork and causes a sudden balance stumble.
7. Ramp / Ukalo (Hill-Start): Stop before the white line using the left (rear) brake. Keep the rear brake firmly squeezed while waiting 2 seconds for the Traffic Police judge's GO signal. To launch with zero rollback: hold rear brake + gently twist throttle until you feel the scooter pull forward, then smoothly release the rear brake!
